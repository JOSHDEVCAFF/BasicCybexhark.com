THE DETAILS, MADE SIMPLE

Frequently asked questions.

A few quick answers about the storefront and checkout preview.

01Can I add products to my cart?

Yes. Use an “Add to cart” button on the shop page. Items and quantities are saved in your browser and appear in checkout.

02Will a real order or payment be placed?

No. This is a front-end demonstration. The payment and confirmation pages only preview the flow; there is no payment processor or order system.

03Should I enter my real card or personal details?

No. Do not enter real payment or personal details. The demo does not need them, send them, or store them.

04Why is my cart empty on another device?

The cart is stored only in this browser on this device. It is not connected to an account or synced between devices.

05How can I ask about a product?

Visit the contact page to email or call Cybexhark. The contact form opens a draft in your email app for you to review.
